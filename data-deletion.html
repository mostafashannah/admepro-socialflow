Data Deletion Instructions

SocialFlow by Admepro stores data from Facebook Pages and Instagram accounts that you connected to it. You can have that data deleted in either of these ways.

Option 1 — Remove the app from Facebook / Instagram

  1. Open Facebook → Settings & privacy → Settings → Business Integrations (for Instagram: Settings → Website permissions / Apps and websites).
  2. Find SocialFlow and click Remove.
  3. Tick the option to delete the data the app received, if offered. SocialFlow loses access immediately.

Option 2 — Email us

Email info@admepro.com with the subject "Delete my data", from the address linked to your account, and tell us the Page or Instagram account name. We will delete the stored tokens, messages, comments and insights for that account and confirm by email within 30 days.

See our Privacy Policy for details on what we store.
