Privacy Policy

SocialFlow by Admepro · Last updated: November 2026

SocialFlow ("the app", at socialflow.admepro.com) is a social media and client management platform operated by Admepro, a marketing agency based in Cairo, Egypt ("we", "us"). It is used by Admepro staff and by the clients who have authorised us to manage their social media accounts. This policy explains what data we handle and why.

1. Data we collect

  • Account data: name, work email and role of staff and client users who sign in to SocialFlow.
  • Facebook and Instagram data (only after the account owner connects their Page or Instagram professional account through Meta's login dialog): Page and account identifiers, names and profile pictures; posts and media we publish for you; comments and direct messages sent to the connected account; and insights such as reach, impressions and follower counts.
  • WhatsApp data: messages sent to or from Admepro's WhatsApp Business number, used for team communication, customer service and notifications.
  • Business data you give us: briefs, creative files, schedules and approvals.

2. How we use it

  • To publish and schedule content on the Pages and Instagram accounts you connect.
  • To show comments and messages in one inbox and let authorised staff reply.
  • To show performance reports (insights) to you and your account manager.
  • To send service notifications and respond to your requests.

We use Meta platform data only to provide these features to the account owner. We do not use it for advertising profiling, we do not sell it, and we do not share it with data brokers or other third parties. Meta data is not used to make decisions about eligibility for credit, employment or insurance.

3. Sharing

Data is visible only to authorised members of the Admepro team and to the client the account belongs to. We use infrastructure and service providers (hosting, email delivery, and AI tools that help draft replies and reports) who process data on our behalf under confidentiality obligations. We disclose data if required by law.

4. Storage and security

Data is stored on servers we control, protected by access control and encrypted connections (HTTPS). Access tokens issued by Meta are stored securely and used only to carry out the actions you authorised. We keep data for as long as the account is connected and as needed to deliver the service, then delete or anonymise it.

5. Your choices and deletion

You can disconnect your Facebook Page or Instagram account at any time from SocialFlow, or remove the app from your Facebook / Instagram settings (Settings → Business Integrations). You can ask us to delete your data at any time by following our Data Deletion Instructions, or by emailing info@admepro.com. We complete deletion requests within 30 days.

6. Children

SocialFlow is a business tool and is not directed to children under 13.

7. Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
